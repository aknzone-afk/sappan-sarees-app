2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Samppan Sarees is the shopping app of a saree house. It lets individual customers anywhere in India browse our designer sarees (zari, thread embroidery, stone and sequin work, Banarasi and organza weaves, bandhej and more), see the price, and order even a single saree for delivery to their door. Audience: adult retail customers in India who buy sarees for themselves and their families.

3. HOW TO USE IT (no demo account needed)
- Open the app: the catalogue is shown immediately, no sign-up.
- Browse the collections or "New In", open a saree, tap CHOOSE COLOUR, tap the saree on the photo and press DONE to add it to the bag.
- Open the bag, tap PLACE ORDER, fill name, mobile (any valid Indian number, e.g. 9876543210), address and pincode (e.g. 110001), then confirm. The order appears in "My Orders". Payment and delivery are arranged by our team outside the app; no payment is taken in the app.
- To delete the account: ☰ > "MY ACCOUNT & DELETE ACCOUNT" > "DELETE MY ACCOUNT" > Delete.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Google Firebase (Cloud Functions, Firestore, Cloud Storage) – our backend for sign-up, orders and account deletion.
- Google Drive – hosts the catalogue photos.
- GitHub Pages – hosts the price list, support page and privacy policy.
- Telegram Bot API – forwards each new order to our shop's private order group so staff can process it.
- Google ML Kit text recognition (on-device) – reads the price printed on catalogue photos so the app can show the correct retail price.
- India Post pincode lookup (api.postalpincode.in) – fills city and state from the pincode on the order form.
There is no in-app payment, no AI content generation, no advertising and no tracking.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app works the same in every region. It is intended for customers in India, because we only deliver within India (prices are in Indian rupees and the order form asks for an Indian mobile number and pincode).

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. We sell our own sarees; all product photos and designs belong to us. The app does not offer financial, medical, gambling or other regulated services.

There is no user-generated content in the app (customers cannot post anything visible to others).
