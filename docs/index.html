THE FINEST WEAVES OF INDIA

Sappan Sarees is a house of luxury sarees inspired by the great weaving traditions of India, delivered to your door anywhere in the country. Browse the collection, choose your colour and place your order from just one saree — all inside the app.

Help with your order

  • Placing an order: open a collection, tap Choose colour, tap the saree you like, press Done, open the bag and press Place order.
  • Your bill and payment: after we confirm your order, your bill appears in the app under Orders. Payment details are shared there.
  • Delivery: dispatch and tracking details appear in Orders as soon as your parcel leaves.
  • Questions about an order: once your bill is sent, a chat opens inside the app for that order.

Account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Orders → ⋮ → Delete my account. See our privacy policy.
