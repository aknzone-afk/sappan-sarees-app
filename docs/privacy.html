Sappan Sarees — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

The Sappan Sarees app lets you browse our saree catalogue and place orders. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

  • Sign-up details you type: your name, mobile number, address and pincode. We use them only to deliver your order and to contact you about it.
  • Your orders: the designs, pieces and any note you add, with photos of the designs you selected.
  • App error reports: technical error messages (no personal content) so we can fix problems.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not show advertising.
  • We do not ask for GST, PAN, Aadhaar or any ID document.

Where it is kept

Orders and sign-up details are stored on Google Firebase (Google Cloud) and sent to our shop's private order group so our staff can pack your order.

Delete your Sappan Sarees account

In the Sappan Sarees app open Orders → ⋮ → Delete my account and confirm. This deletes your account and your sign-up details (name, mobile number, address, pincode) from our servers straight away. Records of orders you already placed are kept for up to 8 years only where Indian tax law requires invoices to be kept, and are never used for anything else.
